Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Privacy First: ZetaNotes processes all AI tasks locally on your device. Your notes never leave your computer unless you explicitly use third-party sync services.

1. What We Collect

We DO NOT collect:

  • Your notes or documents (stored locally only)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Personal files or browsing data

We DO collect:

  • Email address for customer support (optional, only if you contact us)
  • Payment information is processed by Apple through In-App Purchases

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used only to:

  • Provide customer support when requested
  • Support customers with App Store subscription questions

Note: Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store. We do not collect or store payment information.

3. Local AI Processing

All AI features run completely on your device:

  • AI models are downloaded and stored locally
  • Your text never leaves your device for AI processing
  • Works offline - no internet required for AI features
  • No external AI services used (OpenAI, Google, etc.)

4. Third-Party Services

Apple App Store processes payments and manages subscriptions:

  • Payment information is handled entirely by Apple
  • We receive only confirmation of active subscriptions
  • No payment details are shared with or stored by ZetaNotes

View Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • macOS sandbox security (app can only access its own data)
  • Local storage (notes never transmitted)
  • macOS FileVault encryption

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Export your notes at any time
  • Delete all data by uninstalling the app
  • Request account deletion by contacting support

7. Website Analytics

Our website uses Umami Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to understand how visitors use our website:

  • No cookies or tracking pixels
  • No personal data collected
  • No cross-site tracking
  • Only anonymized, aggregated statistics (page views, referral sources)
  • Full GDPR compliance

Important: Website analytics are separate from the app. The ZetaNotes application itself collects NO analytics, telemetry, or usage data.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
