3. Refund Process

How to Request a Refund:

  1. Contact Support: Email rabah.khalek@gmail.com
  2. Provide Details: Include your purchase email and reason for refund
  3. Allow 1-3 Business Days: We'll review and respond quickly

When requesting a refund, please include:

  • Purchase email: Email address used for purchase

3.2 Processing Time

  • Response time: 1-3 business days
