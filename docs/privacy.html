Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2025

Privacy First: ZetaNotes processes all AI tasks locally on your device. Your notes never leave your computer unless you explicitly use third-party sync services.

Privacy at a Glance

Data Type Collected Purpose Storage
Your Notes & Content No Local AI processing only Your device only
Email Address Yes Pro license delivery Paddle (payment processor)
Usage Analytics No Not collected None
Crash Reports Optional Bug fixes (anonymized) Local/Apple (if enabled)

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Your notes and documents: All content remains on your device
  • AI processing data: All AI computations happen locally
  • Usage analytics: No tracking of how you use the app
  • Personal files: No access to other files on your computer
  • Browsing data: No web tracking or cookies
  • Device fingerprinting: No unique device identification

1.2 Information We DO Collect

Purchase Information (Pro Users Only)

  • Email address: Required for license delivery and support
  • Transaction details: Processed by Paddle.com (our payment processor)
  • License key: Generated and stored to validate Pro features

Technical Information

  • App version: For update notifications
  • macOS version: For compatibility checking
  • Crash reports: Only if you explicitly enable them in System Preferences

2. How We Use Information

2.1 Email Address

  • Send Pro license keys after purchase
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important app updates (very rarely)
  • Process refunds if requested

2.2 Technical Information

  • Check for app updates (anonymous requests)
  • Improve app stability through crash analysis
  • Ensure compatibility with your macOS version

3. Local AI Processing

3.1 Complete Privacy

All AI features in ZetaNotes run entirely on your device:

  • Local models: AI models are downloaded and stored on your Mac
  • No external API calls: Your text never leaves your device for AI processing
  • Offline functionality: AI features work without internet connection
  • No cloud processing: We don't use OpenAI, Google, or any external AI services

3.2 Model Downloads

When you first use AI features, ZetaNotes downloads open-source language models:

  • Models are downloaded from Hugging Face (public repository)
  • Downloads are anonymous - no personal information transmitted
  • Models are stored locally in the app's data directory
  • No telemetry is sent about model usage

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Payment Processing

Paddle.com (our merchant of record) receives:

  • Your email address
  • Payment information (credit card, etc.)
  • Billing address
  • Purchase details

Paddle's privacy policy: https://paddle.com/privacy

4.2 App Updates

GitHub hosts our update feed:

  • Anonymous requests to check for updates
  • No personal information transmitted
  • Standard web server logs may be kept by GitHub

4.3 No Other Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Share data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing
  • Provide data to analytics companies

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

  • Your notes: Stored in ZetaNotes' macOS sandbox
  • AI models: Cached locally for offline use
  • App settings: Stored in macOS preferences
  • License information: Stored securely in macOS Keychain

5.2 Data Security

  • Sandbox protection: App can only access its own data
  • macOS security: Protected by macOS file permissions
  • No network exposure: Local data never transmitted
  • Encrypted storage: Benefits from macOS FileVault encryption

6. Your Rights and Controls

6.1 Data Access

  • Your notes: Always accessible through the app
  • Export options: Export notes to standard formats
  • Purchase history: Available through Paddle customer portal

6.2 Data Deletion

  • Uninstall app: Removes all local data
  • Reset app: Clear all data through app preferences
  • Account deletion: Contact support to remove purchase records

6.3 Communication Preferences

  • Opt out of support emails (except transactional)
  • Update notification settings in app preferences
  • Crash report sharing in macOS System Preferences

7. Children's Privacy

ZetaNotes is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us to delete it.

8. International Users

ZetaNotes is available worldwide. Since all AI processing happens locally:

  • Your data never crosses borders for AI processing
  • Only payment information is shared with Paddle (EU/US)
  • GDPR compliance through minimal data collection
  • Local data sovereignty maintained

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of ZetaNotes after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach involving your purchase information:

  • We will notify affected users within 72 hours
  • Details about the breach and steps taken will be provided
  • Recommendations for protecting your account will be shared
  • Note: Your notes and AI data cannot be breached as they never leave your device
